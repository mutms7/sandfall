A FIELD GUIDE

Make yourself at home.

Choose an ingredient, then draw in the world. Materials react, people find their own way, and nothing needs a score.

Move around

Choose the hand tool and drag, tap a region, or drag the world map. A mouse wheel and A / D work too.

Change things

Hold to pour materials. Drag to add people or wildlife. Use the eraser, or right-drag, to make space.

Take your time

Pause to build, then watch it unfold. Step advances one tick. Speed controls materials, people, wildlife, and Life patterns together.

Keep a world

Save up to eight worlds here. Clear, New world, and removing creatures offer an undo until your next world change.
